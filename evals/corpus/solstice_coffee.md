# Solstice Coffee Roasters: Company Profile

Solstice Coffee Roasters was founded in 2008 in Wellington, New Zealand, by
Tomas Reiner, a former marine engineer, and Ana Quispe, a third-generation
coffee farmer from the Cusco region of Peru. The pair met at a trade fair
and bonded over a shared frustration: excellent high-altitude Peruvian lots
were routinely blended away into anonymity.

## Roastery and Equipment

The company's roastery is located in Petone, on the northern shore of
Wellington Harbour. All production runs on a single Loring S70 Peregrine,
a 70-kilogram smart roaster chosen for its low gas consumption and
repeatable profiles. The roastery operates four days a week, and every
batch is cupped by at least two graders before release.

After roasting, all Solstice beans rest for 9 days before they are shipped
or brewed. The company considers this resting window essential for its
espresso blends and prints a "ready from" date, rather than a roast date,
on every bag.

## Signature Coffees

The flagship espresso blend is called Long Dusk and is composed of
60% Peruvian beans from the Quillabamba valley and 40% Ethiopian beans
from the Guji zone. Long Dusk has anchored the menu since 2011 and accounts
for nearly half of all sales.

The company's most decorated coffee is Quillabamba Gold, a honey-processed
single origin grown on Ana Quispe's family farm. Quillabamba Gold won the
Golden Bean roasting competition in 2022 in the honey-processed category,
the first win for a New Zealand roaster in that division.

## Retail and People

Solstice operates 14 cafes: nine across the Wellington region, four in
Auckland, and one in Christchurch. The company employs 312 people across
its cafes, roastery, and support office. Cafe staff complete a six-week
sensory training program before serving on bar.

## Subscriptions

Home drinkers can join the Orbit Club, the company's subscription service,
which ships a rotating selection of two coffees every 14 days. Orbit Club
members receive each release one week before it reaches retail shelves, and
the subscription can be paused from the company's app at any time.
