# Helios MK-3 Solar Panel: Owner's Maintenance Guide

This guide covers routine care of the Helios MK-3 photovoltaic panel and
its companion inverter. Following the schedule below keeps the system
within its warranty terms.

## Panel Specifications

The Helios MK-3 is a bifacial monocrystalline panel with a rated output of
460 watts and a conversion efficiency of 23.1%. Each panel weighs
24 kilograms and is rated for an operating temperature range of -40°C to
85°C. Expected performance degradation is 0.45% per year, and the MK-3
carries a 28-year linear power output warranty — among the longest in its
class. The warranty is voided by unapproved cleaning methods or by
operating the panel outside its rated temperature range.

## Cleaning

Clean the panels every 6 months, or quarterly in regions with heavy dust,
pollen, or coastal salt spray. Use only a soft brush and deionized water.
Never use a pressure washer, abrasive pads, or detergent: pressure washing
can force water past the edge seals and is the most common cause of
warranty-voiding damage. Clean early in the morning while panels are cool;
spraying cold water on hot glass can cause micro-cracking.

## Mounting Hardware

Inspect mounting hardware once a year. All mounting bolts on the standard
HRail system must be torqued to 18 newton-meters. Over-tightening can
deform the frame and crack cells along the panel edge, while loose bolts
allow vibration wear on the rail anodizing. Replace any bolt showing
corrosion with the stainless part HB-18 only.

## Inverter and Fault Codes

The MK-3 is designed to pair with the SunCore X9 string inverter. The X9
reports faults on its front display and in the companion app. The two codes
owners encounter most often are:

- **E-42** — ground fault. The inverter has detected leakage current to
  earth. Shut the system down at the DC isolator and contact a licensed
  installer; do not reset and continue operating.
- **E-17** — grid overvoltage. The utility grid voltage has drifted above
  the inverter's window. This usually clears on its own; if it recurs
  daily, ask your utility to check the local transformer tap settings.

## Firmware

Inverter and panel-optimizer firmware is updated through the HeliosLink
mobile app. Check for firmware updates every 90 days. Updates are applied
at night so that daytime generation is not interrupted, and the system
keeps the previous firmware image for automatic rollback.
