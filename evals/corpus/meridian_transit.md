# The MetroLink System of Meridian City

MetroLink is the rapid transit network serving Meridian City and its inner
suburbs. The system opened to the public in 1987 with a single eleven-station
line and has since grown into one of the densest mid-sized metro networks in
the region.

## Lines

MetroLink operates five lines, each identified by a color name: the Amber
Line, the Cobalt Line, the Jade Line, the Scarlet Line, and the Onyx Line.

The longest of the five is the Cobalt Line, which runs 42 kilometers from
Harborfront in the south to Birch Hill in the northern suburbs and serves
31 stations. The shortest is the Onyx Line, a 9-kilometer downtown loop.

The Jade Line became the system's first fully driverless line when it was
converted to automated operation in 2019. Conversion of the Scarlet Line to
driverless operation is planned but has not been scheduled.

## Fares and Ridership

MetroLink uses a flat-fare model: a single ride costs 2.75 regardless of
distance traveled, paid with the contactless OrbitCard or by tapping a bank
card at the gate. Children under eight travel free with a fare-paying adult.

Average weekday ridership across the network is 1.4 million passenger
journeys, with the Cobalt Line alone accounting for roughly a third of all
trips. The busiest single station is Union Concourse, where the Amber,
Cobalt, and Onyx lines meet.

## Service Hours

Regular service runs from 5:00 a.m. to midnight, seven days a week. On
Friday and Saturday nights, MetroLink extends service on all five lines
until 3:00 a.m. to serve the entertainment districts along the Scarlet
Line. During extended night service, trains run every fifteen minutes.

## Expansion

The current expansion program, known as Project Horizon, will add a sixth
line — the Violet Line — connecting Meridian International Airport to Union
Concourse. Project Horizon broke ground in 2026, and the Violet Line is
scheduled to open to passengers by 2030. The project also adds three infill
stations on the Amber Line and a new maintenance depot at Birch Hill.
