# Borealis BX-2 Solar Panel: Owner's Maintenance Guide

This guide covers routine care of the Borealis BX-2 photovoltaic panel.
The BX-2 is a budget-oriented alternative to premium panels such as the
Helios MK-3, and its maintenance requirements differ in several ways.

## Panel Specifications

The Borealis BX-2 is a monofacial polycrystalline panel with a rated
output of 415 watts and a conversion efficiency of 21.4%. Each panel
weighs 21 kilograms. Expected performance degradation is 0.6% per year,
and the BX-2 carries a 25-year linear power output warranty.

## Cleaning

Clean BX-2 panels every 12 months. Unlike premium panels, the BX-2's
hardened glass coating tolerates a garden hose on a gentle setting,
although a soft brush and plain water remain the recommended method.
Pressure washers should still be avoided.

## Mounting Hardware

Inspect mounting hardware every two years. All mounting bolts on the
Borealis QuickRail system must be torqued to 14 newton-meters. Use only
the supplied galvanized hardware; mixing stainless and galvanized parts
causes galvanic corrosion at the rail joints.

## Inverter and Fault Codes

The BX-2 pairs with the Borealis Wave-5 string inverter. Its two most
common fault codes are:

- **F-11** — DC isolation fault. Shut down the array and have the wiring
  inspected by an installer.
- **F-30** — over-temperature. The inverter has throttled output because
  its heatsink exceeded limits; clear debris around the unit and ensure
  shade ventilation.

## Firmware

Wave-5 firmware updates are delivered automatically over the inverter's
Wi-Fi connection; no schedule or companion app is required.
