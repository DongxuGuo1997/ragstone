# Meridian Transit Green Line Extension: Project Overview

The Meridian Transit Authority is proud to announce the Green Line extension, a major expansion of our urban transit network designed to enhance connectivity and reduce travel times across the city. This document provides an overview of the project, including key specifications, station details, fare structure, and anticipated opening dates.

## Project Specifications

The Green Line extension will add 15.4 kilometers of track to the existing network, connecting the northern district of Arbor Heights to the southern hub of Crescent Bay. The project includes the construction of 12 new stations, each equipped with state-of-the-art facilities to ensure passenger comfort and safety. The extension will utilize the new MT-5000 series trains, which boast a top speed of 110 km/h and a passenger capacity of 1,200 per train set. These trains are powered by the latest EcoDrive technology, reducing energy consumption by 18% compared to previous models.

## Station Details

The Green Line extension will feature the following new stations:

1. Arbor Heights Central
2. Maplewood Park
3. Eastwood Junction
4. Riverbend
5. Greenfield Plaza
6. Midtown Exchange
7. Westgate
8. Southport
9. Oceanview
10. Crescent Bay North
11. Crescent Bay Central
12. Crescent Bay South

Each station is designed with accessibility in mind, including elevators, tactile paving, and audio announcements. The stations will also feature solar-powered lighting systems, utilizing the Helios MK-3 panels to ensure sustainable energy use.

## Fare Structure

The Green Line extension will implement a new fare structure distinct from the existing Violet Line. Single-ride tickets will be priced at $3.75, with discounts available for students, seniors, and frequent travelers. Monthly passes will be offered at $95, providing unlimited travel across the entire Meridian Transit network. The introduction of contactless payment options will streamline the boarding process and reduce wait times.

## Construction Timeline and Opening Dates

Construction of the Green Line extension began in April 2032, with an expected completion date in late 2035. The phased opening of stations will commence in March 2035, starting with Arbor Heights Central and concluding with Crescent Bay South by December 2035. This staggered approach will allow for thorough testing and integration with the existing network, ensuring a seamless transition for passengers.

## Environmental and Community Impact

The Green Line extension is committed to minimizing its environmental footprint and enhancing community engagement. The project includes the planting of over 5,000 trees along the route, contributing to urban greening and air quality improvement. Community feedback sessions have been held quarterly since the project's inception, allowing residents to voice concerns and suggestions. The Meridian Transit Authority is dedicated to maintaining transparency and fostering a collaborative relationship with the communities we serve.

In summary, the Green Line extension represents a significant investment in the future of urban transit, promising improved connectivity, sustainability, and passenger experience. We look forward to welcoming you aboard as we continue to build a more connected city.
