# Meridian Transit System: Fare Policy Handbook

Welcome to the Meridian Transit System, your reliable partner in urban mobility. This handbook outlines our fare policies, ensuring a smooth and efficient travel experience for all passengers. Please familiarize yourself with the following sections to make the most of our services.

## Fare Structure

The Meridian Transit System offers a variety of fare options to suit different travel needs. Our fare structure is designed to be both flexible and economical:

- **Single-Trip Fare**: $2.75 for adults, $1.50 for children (ages 5-12), and $1.00 for seniors (65+).
- **Day Pass**: Unlimited travel for a day at $7.00 for adults and $4.00 for children and seniors.
- **Weekly Pass**: $25.00 for adults and $15.00 for children and seniors, providing unlimited travel for seven consecutive days.
- **Monthly Pass**: $90.00 for adults and $50.00 for children and seniors, valid for unlimited travel throughout the month.

## Concessions and Discounts

Meridian Transit offers several concessions to ensure accessibility for all passengers:

- **Student Discount**: Students aged 13-18 receive a 20% discount on all fare types with a valid student ID.
- **Family Pass**: Families traveling together (up to 2 adults and 3 children) can purchase a Family Day Pass for $15.00.
- **Off-Peak Discount**: Travel during off-peak hours (10 AM - 3 PM and 7 PM - 5 AM) to receive a 10% discount on single-trip fares.

## Fare Capping

To provide cost-effective travel, Meridian Transit employs a fare capping system:

- **Daily Cap**: No passenger will be charged more than $8.00 per day, regardless of the number of trips taken.
- **Weekly Cap**: Total fares will not exceed $30.00 in any given week.
- **Monthly Cap**: A maximum of $100.00 will be charged in a calendar month, ensuring predictable travel expenses.

## Penalties and Fines

To maintain a fair and orderly system, the following penalties apply for fare violations:

- **Fare Evasion**: A fine of $150.00 is imposed for traveling without a valid ticket or pass.
- **Invalid Concession Use**: Misuse of concession fares results in a $75.00 fine.
- **Tampering with Fare Equipment**: A serious offense with a fine of $500.00 and potential legal action.

## Ticketing and Payment

Meridian Transit offers multiple convenient payment methods:

- **Contactless Cards**: Use your credit or debit card for quick and easy fare payment.
- **Meridian App**: Download our app to purchase and store digital tickets on your smartphone.
- **Ticket Vending Machines**: Available at all major stations, accepting cash, credit, and debit payments.

For any questions or assistance, please contact our customer service team at 1-800-MERIDIAN or visit our website at www.meridiantransit.com. Thank you for choosing Meridian Transit, where your journey is our priority.
