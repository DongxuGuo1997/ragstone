# SunCore X10 Inverter: Service and Maintenance Manual

The SunCore X10 inverter is the latest advancement in solar energy conversion technology, succeeding the popular SunCore X9 model. This manual provides detailed instructions for the maintenance and troubleshooting of the X10, ensuring optimal performance and longevity.

## Specifications

The SunCore X10 is a high-efficiency string inverter designed for residential and commercial solar installations. It boasts a maximum output of 12,000 watts and operates with a conversion efficiency of 98.7%. The inverter is compatible with a wide range of photovoltaic panels, including the Helios MK-3. The X10 is rated for an operating temperature range of -30°C to 75°C and features an IP65 enclosure, making it suitable for outdoor installations.

- **Model Number**: SC-X10-12K
- **Weight**: 32 kilograms
- **Dimensions**: 600 mm x 400 mm x 200 mm
- **Cooling**: Active cooling with dual variable-speed fans
- **Warranty**: 15-year limited warranty

## Installation and Mounting

The SunCore X10 must be mounted vertically on a solid surface, using the provided mounting bracket. Ensure that the inverter is installed at least 1 meter above ground level to prevent water ingress. Use only the SunCore MB-10 mounting bracket and secure with bolts torqued to 20 newton-meters. Over-tightening can damage the mounting bracket, while under-tightening may lead to vibration and noise.

## Fault Codes and Troubleshooting

The X10 features an advanced diagnostic system that displays fault codes on its LCD screen and in the SunCoreLink app. Familiarity with these codes is essential for timely troubleshooting:

- **F-51** — DC input overcurrent. This indicates that the current from the solar array exceeds the inverter's input capacity. Disconnect the array and inspect for wiring faults or panel mismatches.
- **F-23** — internal temperature high. The inverter's cooling system is unable to maintain safe operating temperatures. Check for obstructions to airflow and ensure that the fans are functioning properly.
- **F-19** — grid undervoltage. The utility grid voltage has fallen below the inverter's operational window. This typically resolves itself; if persistent, contact your utility provider.

## Firmware Updates

Firmware updates for the SunCore X10 are managed through the SunCoreLink mobile app. It is recommended to check for updates every 60 days. Updates are applied automatically during nighttime hours to avoid disruption of daytime energy production. The system retains the previous firmware version for 30 days, allowing for rollback in case of update issues.

## Maintenance Schedule

To ensure the SunCore X10 operates efficiently, adhere to the following maintenance schedule:

- **Monthly**: Inspect the inverter for physical damage and ensure that the cooling fans are operational.
- **Quarterly**: Clean the inverter's exterior with a soft cloth and check the mounting bolts for tightness.
- **Annually**: Conduct a comprehensive system check, including firmware updates, fault code logs, and performance metrics review.

By following this service manual, users can maximize the performance and lifespan of their SunCore X10 inverter, ensuring reliable solar energy conversion for years to come.
