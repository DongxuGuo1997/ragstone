# Helios MK-4 Solar Panel: Product Announcement

Introducing the Helios MK-4, the latest evolution in our line of high-performance solar panels. Building on the success of the Helios MK-3, the MK-4 offers enhanced efficiency, increased power output, and improved durability, making it the ideal choice for both residential and commercial solar installations.

## Panel Specifications

The Helios MK-4 is designed with cutting-edge bifacial heterojunction technology, delivering a remarkable rated output of 495 watts and a conversion efficiency of 24.3%. Each panel weighs 26 kilograms, slightly heavier than its predecessor, due to the reinforced frame designed to withstand extreme weather conditions. The MK-4 operates efficiently in a temperature range from -45°C to 90°C, ensuring robust performance in diverse climates. The panel's performance degradation is minimized to just 0.35% per year, and it comes with a 30-year linear power output warranty, setting a new benchmark in the industry.

## Cleaning and Maintenance

To maintain optimal performance, clean the Helios MK-4 panels every 4 months, or more frequently in areas with high levels of dust, pollen, or salt exposure. Use a soft microfiber cloth and distilled water to avoid damaging the panel surface. Avoid using high-pressure water jets, abrasive materials, or chemical detergents, as these can compromise the panel's integrity and void the warranty. Cleaning should be performed during cooler parts of the day to prevent thermal shock to the glass surface.

## Mounting Hardware

The Helios MK-4 utilizes the advanced V-Rail mounting system, which requires all bolts to be torqued to 20 newton-meters. This precise torque specification ensures the stability of the panel while preventing damage to the frame and solar cells. Regularly inspect the mounting hardware for signs of wear or corrosion, and replace any compromised components with the V-Grade stainless steel bolts (model VG-20) to maintain structural integrity.

## Inverter Compatibility and Fault Codes

The MK-4 is optimized for use with the SunCore X10 string inverter, which features enhanced diagnostic capabilities. Common fault codes include:

- **E-50** — isolation fault. Indicates a potential insulation breakdown. Immediately shut down the system and consult a certified technician for inspection.
- **E-22** — frequency deviation. Occurs when the grid frequency is outside the inverter's operational range. This issue often resolves automatically, but persistent occurrences should be reported to the utility provider.

## Firmware and Connectivity

Stay up-to-date with the latest firmware for both the inverter and panel optimizers through the HeliosConnect app. Firmware updates are released biannually, ensuring your system benefits from the latest advancements in solar technology. Updates are scheduled during nighttime hours to prevent any disruption to daytime energy production. The system retains the previous firmware version to facilitate automatic rollback if necessary.
