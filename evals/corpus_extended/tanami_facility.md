# Tanami Curation Facility: Operational Procedures Manual

This manual outlines the operational protocols for the Tanami Curation Facility, responsible for the storage and analysis of extraterrestrial samples, including the Aurora-7 collection. Adherence to these procedures ensures the integrity and security of the samples.

## Facility Overview

The Tanami Curation Facility, established in 2032, is located in the remote Tanami Desert, providing an ideal environment for the secure storage of sensitive materials. The facility spans 3,200 square meters and includes state-of-the-art laboratories, climate-controlled storage vaults, and advanced security systems. The facility is staffed by 42 personnel, including scientists, engineers, and security staff.

## Sample Storage Specifications

The Aurora-7 samples, weighing 850 grams and collected from Site Juniper, are stored in the facility's primary vault. The vault maintains a constant temperature of -196°C, using liquid nitrogen cooling systems to preserve the samples' pristine condition. Temperature fluctuations are kept within ±0.5°C to prevent any degradation. The storage vault is equipped with Model TCF-9 cryogenic containment units, each capable of holding up to 200 grams of material. The Aurora-7 samples are distributed across five units, ensuring redundancy and minimizing risk.

## Access and Security Protocols

Access to the storage vault is strictly controlled and limited to authorized personnel only. Entry requires dual authentication: a biometric scan and a personalized access code. The facility employs a three-tier security system, including 24/7 surveillance, motion detectors, and automated lockdown procedures in case of unauthorized access attempts. Security audits are conducted quarterly, with the next scheduled for December 15, 2034.

## Sample Handling Procedures

Handling of the Aurora-7 samples is restricted to certified personnel only, who must wear protective suits and use sterilized tools to prevent contamination. All handling activities are logged in the Sample Management System (SMS-7), which tracks sample movement and usage. The facility follows a strict protocol for sample analysis, requiring all tests to be conducted in the Class 100 cleanroom environment. The cleanroom is maintained at a positive pressure of 12 Pascals to prevent particulate ingress.

## Maintenance and Calibration

The facility's equipment undergoes regular maintenance to ensure optimal performance. The cryogenic systems are inspected monthly, with the next inspection due on November 10, 2034. Calibration of the temperature sensors is performed biannually, with the last calibration completed on August 20, 2034. The facility's backup power systems, including the Helios MK-3 solar panels, are cleaned and inspected every 6 months, with the next cleaning scheduled for January 5, 2035.

The Tanami Curation Facility is committed to maintaining the highest standards of sample preservation and security, ensuring the valuable Aurora-7 samples remain in perfect condition for future research and exploration.
