# Corona K-7 Solar Panel: Owner's Maintenance Guide

This guide provides essential maintenance instructions for the Corona K-7 photovoltaic panel and its compatible inverter system. Adhering to the guidelines below ensures optimal performance and longevity of your solar investment.

## Panel Specifications

The Corona K-7 is a high-efficiency polycrystalline panel with a rated output of 475 watts and a conversion efficiency of 22.7%. Each panel weighs 26 kilograms and is engineered to operate within a temperature range of -35°C to 90°C. The expected performance degradation is 0.50% per year. The K-7 comes with a 30-year linear power output warranty, which is among the longest available. The warranty is voided by unauthorized cleaning methods or if the panel is operated outside its specified temperature range.

## Cleaning

Clean the panels every 4 months, or every 2 months in areas with significant dust, pollen, or marine salt exposure. Use only a microfiber cloth and distilled water. Avoid using high-pressure washers, abrasive materials, or chemical detergents, as these can damage the panel surface and void the warranty. Clean the panels in the early morning or late afternoon when they are cool to prevent thermal shock, which can lead to micro-fractures.

## Mounting Hardware

Inspect the mounting hardware biannually. All mounting bolts on the standard KTrack system should be torqued to 16 newton-meters. Over-tightening can lead to frame deformation and potential cell cracking, while under-tightening may result in vibrational damage to the rail coating. Replace any corroded bolts with the stainless steel part CB-16 only.

## Inverter and Fault Codes

The K-7 is designed to work seamlessly with the SolarMax Y12 string inverter. Faults are displayed on the inverter's front panel and within the SolarMax mobile app. Common fault codes include:

- **E-44** — insulation resistance fault. The inverter has detected a drop in insulation resistance. Immediately shut down the system using the DC disconnect and contact a certified technician; do not attempt to restart the system.
- **E-19** — grid undervoltage. The utility grid voltage has fallen below the inverter's operational threshold. This issue often resolves itself; if it persists, contact your utility provider to assess the local grid conditions.

## Firmware

Firmware updates for the inverter and panel optimizers are managed through the CoronaLink mobile application. It is recommended to check for updates every 120 days. Updates are applied automatically during nighttime hours to avoid disruption of daytime energy production. The system retains the previous firmware version to facilitate an automatic rollback if needed.

By following these maintenance guidelines, you can ensure that your Corona K-7 solar panel system operates efficiently and remains within warranty terms for its full lifespan.
